Applying a proposal

What it takes to apply a TC39 proposal to this specification, shown on tc39/proposal-defer-import-eval: the text merged, what was written by hand, what was regenerated, what the merge found, and the results.

What was done

The proposal tc39/proposal-defer-import-eval, at its commit f19b6c5, was applied to ECMA-262 at b1eaed82f in a branch of this repository, proposal-defer-import-eval (acbd03d, from 3a2102a): the proposal's text merged into the specification's, the Lean specification regenerated from the result, the engine built and its claims checked, and the proposal's test262 tests run.

  • 1,042lines added by hand (−211 removed) across 24 files: the pin, 10 merge answers, SpecTec definitions & HTML metadata (including 14 step readings), and tests
  • 0files of hand-written Lean changed
  • 251of the proposal's 252 test262 tests (import-defer) pass; 1 not run, none fails

How a proposal is applied

  1. Pin it. The proposal's repository is a submodule, external/proposal-defer-import-eval, at f19b6c5, and one line of spec/proposals.txt names it with its answers file.
  2. Merge it. scripts/apply_proposal.py reads the proposal's spec.emu as a diff against ECMA-262: it matches each clause by its id and each block of the clause by its old text (the block with its <ins> dropped and its <del> kept), which must be the base's; it inserts the proposal's new clauses and removes the ones it deletes. Where the old text is not the base's, or an inserted block's place is not shown, it refuses and asks: the answers file (spec/proposals/defer-import-eval/resolutions.txt) answers each question, one line each.
  3. The text. scripts/spec_text.py makes the text every generator, check and page reads: ECMA-262 with the proposals of spec/proposals.txt applied, remade whenever an input changes.
  4. SpecTec. crates/spectec-html translates new or changed algorithms, grammar rules, tables and early errors into spec/spectec/ and checks the round-trip back to the merged spec.html.
  5. Regenerate. python3 scripts/generate.py runs spectec emit over spec/spectec/ to write lean/Ecma/Gen and lean/Ecma/Grammar/Gen (python3 scripts/spec_text.py --report says what the merge did).
  6. Build and check. lake build, the axiom check of the claims, python3 scripts/quick_check.py, and test262 with the proposal's feature no longer in [skip-features].

What regenerating changed

Generated Lean modules (lean/Ecma/Gen and lean/Ecma/Grammar/Gen) are gitignored and emitted directly from spec/spectec/ by scripts/generate.py.

Every identifier in SpecTec is symbolic (How it works); crates/spectec derives sorted numeric indices and Ecma.Known constants automatically when emitting Lean, so diffs stay confined to the changed SpecTec definitions and downstream proofs continue to name the same declarations.

With the answers, it changes 26 clauses, inserts 11, removes 2 and copies 3 figures; every one of the proposal's 129 <ins> texts is in the result, none of its 57 <del> texts is left, and the clause tree is the base's less the removed clauses plus the inserted ones.

For the merge to ask nothing, a proposal's text would reproduce whole blocks (no abbreviated headers; an inserted note shown with the algorithm before it), be written against the current ECMA-262, mark every change with <ins> and <del>, and rename a removed operation at every call or say that the rest are renamed with it.
