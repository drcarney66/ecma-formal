65. Date.prototype.toISOString leaves 4-digit vs expanded ±YYYYYY year format unspecified for 0..9999

Location
§21.4.4.36 Date.prototype.toISOString (#sec-date.prototype.toisostring), step 7, with §21.4.1.32 Date Time String Format (#sec-date-time-string-format), YYYY and Expanded Years.
Kind
underspecification (test262 and all engines require 4-digit YYYY for years 0..9999, whereas the prose permits +002026).
Model erratum
ToISOStringYearForm, baked-in SpecTec reading test262 ($ExpandedYearForm in spec/spectec/clauses/S21.spectec and lean/Engine/Host/Core.lean). SpecTec binds $ExpandedYearForm to false for years in 0..9999 so toISOString always emits 4-digit YYYY there and 6-digit ±YYYYYY outside 0..9999.
Upstream
Checked 2026-11-19: none found (searched for "toISOString" with "expanded year").
On this site
ToISOStringYearForm on Spec issues & errata (defect of the text; SpecTec bakes in the test262 reading)

Text

Date.prototype.toISOString:

7. Return a String representation of tv in the Date Time String Format on the UTC time scale, including all format elements and the UTC offset representation "Z".

§21.4.1.32:

YYYY is the year in the proleptic Gregorian calendar as four decimal digits from 0000 to 9999, or as an expanded year of "+" or "-" followed by six decimal digits.

Problem

§21.4.1.32 defines the input/interchange grammar accepted by Date.parse, where +002026-01-01T00:00:00.000Z is a valid expanded-year representation of 2026 (only -000000 is forbidden). Date.prototype.toISOString step 7 asks for "a String representation of tv in the Date Time String Format" without restricting expanded years to < 0 or > 9999, leaving "+002026-01-01T00:00:00.000Z" a literally conforming result for new Date(Date.UTC(2026, 0, 1)).toISOString().

Examples

print(new Date(Date.UTC(0, 0, 1)).toISOString());
print(new Date(Date.UTC(2026, 0, 1)).toISOString());
print(new Date(Date.UTC(10000, 0, 1)).toISOString());
year 1900 (Date.UTC(0, 0, 1)) year 2026 year 10000
literal text "1900-..." or "+001900-..." "2026-..." or "+002026-..." "+010000-01-01T00:00:00.000Z"
V8, SpiderMonkey, JavaScriptCore "1900-01-01T00:00:00.000Z" "2026-01-01T00:00:00.000Z" "+010000-01-01T00:00:00.000Z"
formal model (test262 reading) "1900-01-01T00:00:00.000Z" "2026-01-01T00:00:00.000Z" "+010000-01-01T00:00:00.000Z"

Proposed wording

Add to Date.prototype.toISOString step 7: "representing the year as four digits if 0 ≤ YearFromTime(tv) ≤ 9999 and as an expanded year otherwise."

test262

built-ins/Date/prototype/toISOString/15.9.5.43-0-4.js and 15.9.1.15-1.js require exact 4-digit YYYY strings for years in 0..9999.
