55. RegExpUnicodeEscapeSequence: escaped surrogate pairs are ambiguous in the formal grammar

Location
§22.2.1 Patterns (#sec-patterns), RegExpUnicodeEscapeSequence, with §5.1.6.7 Lookahead Restrictions (#sec-lookahead-restrictions).
Kind
ambiguity in the formal context-free grammar (an incomplete prose disambiguation sentence outside §5.1.6).
Model erratum
RegExpUnicodeEscapeLookahead, baked-in SpecTec reading test262 (layer parser, spec/html/grammar-meta.txt and spec/spectec/04-grammar-regexp.spectec). SpecTec attaches a 16-prefix lookahead exclusion ([lookahead ∉ { "\uDC", …, "\udf" }]) to [+UnicodeMode] u HexLeadSurrogate so greedy surrogate pairing is unambiguous in the formal grammar and ParseLaws.complete holds.
Upstream
Checked 2026-11-19: none found (searched for "RegExpUnicodeEscapeSequence" and "HexLeadSurrogate").
On this site
RegExpUnicodeEscapeLookahead on Spec issues & errata (defect of the text; SpecTec bakes in the test262 reading)

Text

§22.2.1:

RegExpUnicodeEscapeSequence[UnicodeMode] :: [+UnicodeMode] u HexLeadSurrogate \u HexTrailSurrogate [+UnicodeMode] u HexLeadSurrogate [+UnicodeMode] u HexTrailSurrogate [+UnicodeMode] u HexNonSurrogate [~UnicodeMode] u Hex4Digits [+UnicodeMode] u{ CodePoint }

Each \\\\u HexTrailSurrogate for which the choice of associated u HexLeadSurrogate is ambiguous shall be associated with the nearest possible u HexLeadSurrogate that would otherwise have no corresponding \\\\u HexTrailSurrogate.

Problem

Under §5.1's context-free derivation relation, RegExpUnicodeEscapeSequence[+UnicodeMode] has both u HexLeadSurrogate \u HexTrailSurrogate and unguarded u HexLeadSurrogate alongside u HexTrailSurrogate. Every escaped surrogate pair in [+UnicodeMode] — and in RegExpIdentifierStart / RegExpIdentifierPart even without /u or /v, since group names invoke RegExpUnicodeEscapeSequence[+UnicodeMode] unconditionally — therefore admits two parse trees with observably different semantics:

  • /\uD800\uDC00+/u: one quantified astral atom U+10000 vs U+D800 followed by quantified U+DC00+;
  • /[\uD800\uDC00]/u: CharSet { U+10000 } (matching "\u{10000}") vs { 0xD800, 0xDC00 };
  • /(?<\uD835\uDC9C>a)/: valid astral ID_Start group name vs two lone surrogates (an early SyntaxError).

The prose sentence after the grammar lies outside §5.1.6's formal grammar notation and literally only resolves which lead surrogate a trail surrogate pairs with when multiple leads compete — presupposing, rather than stating, that \u |HexTrailSurrogate| cannot remain unassociated and parse via [+UnicodeMode] u HexTrailSurrogate.

Examples

print(/\uD800\uDC00+/u.test("\u{10000}\u{10000}"));
print(/[\uD800\uDC00]/u.test("\u{10000}"));
print(/(?<\uD835\uDC9C>a)/.exec("a").groups["\u{1D49C}"]);
/\uD800\uDC00+/u /[\uD800\uDC00]/u /(?<\uD835\uDC9C>a)/
formal grammar without lookahead ambiguous (2 parses) ambiguous (2 parses) ambiguous (valid vs SyntaxError)
V8, SpiderMonkey, JavaScriptCore true true "a"
formal model (test262 reading) true true "a"

Proposed wording

Replace the prose sentence with a formal §5.1.6.7 lookahead exclusion [lookahead ∉ BackslashHexTrailSurrogate] (where BackslashHexTrailSurrogate :: \u HexTrailSurrogate) on [+UnicodeMode] u HexLeadSurrogate.

test262

built-ins/RegExp/unicode_restricted_quantifiable_assertion.js, language/literals/regexp/u-surrogate-pairs*.js and language/literals/regexp/named-groups/ require greedy surrogate pairing.
