62. CountLeftCapturingParensWithin step 1 fails its #sec-patterns Assert: on every quantified §B.1.2 term

Location
§22.2.1.2 Static Semantics: CountLeftCapturingParensWithin (#sec-countleftcapturingparenswithin), step 1, with §B.1.2.2 CountLeftCapturingParensWithin (#sec-countleftcapturingparens-annexb) and §B.1.2 (#sec-regular-expressions-patterns).
Kind
failing Assert (every quantified ExtendedAtom or QuantifiableAssertion under §B.1.2 fails step 1).
Model erratum
AnnexBCapturingParensAssert, baked-in SpecTec reading test262 (clause_prods @sec-patterns in spec/spectec/00-decls.spectec and S22.spectec). SpecTec includes the 36 Annex B RegExp productions in clause_prods @sec-patterns so $InstanceOf(parseNode, clause @sec-patterns) succeeds, and dispatches ParsePattern and CompileToCharSet to §B.1.2 when the host supports @sec-regular-expressions-patterns.
Upstream

On this site
AnnexBCapturingParensAssert on Spec issues & errata (defect of the text; SpecTec bakes in the test262 reading)

Text

§22.2.1.2:

1. Assert: parseNode is an instance of a production in the RegExp Pattern grammar.

2. Return the number of Atom :: ( GroupSpecifier? Disjunction ) Parse Nodes contained within parseNode.

(where "the RegExp Pattern grammar" is <emu-xref href="#sec-patterns">). §B.1.2.2:

In the definitions of CountLeftCapturingParensWithin and CountLeftCapturingParensBefore, references to “Atom :: ( GroupSpecifier? Disjunction )” are to be interpreted as meaning “Atom :: ( GroupSpecifier? Disjunction )” or “ExtendedAtom :: ( GroupSpecifier? Disjunction )”.

Problem

Under §B.1.2 (#sec-regular-expressions-patterns), every quantified atom parses as Term :: ExtendedAtom Quantifier or Term :: QuantifiableAssertion Quantifier, and CompileSubpattern passes that |ExtendedAtom| or |QuantifiableAssertion| node to CountLeftCapturingParensWithin. §B.1.2.2 updates step 2 to count ExtendedAtom groups, but forgets to widen step 1's Assert: from #sec-patterns to include #sec-regular-expressions-patterns — so every quantified term under §B.1.2 (even /a+/) falsifies step 1's Assert:!

Examples

print(/a+/.test("aa"), /(a)+/.exec("aa")[1]);
/a+/.test("aa") under §B.1.2 /(a)+/.exec("aa")[1] under §B.1.2
literal step 1 Assert: fails (ExtendedAtom is in #sec-regular-expressions-patterns, not #sec-patterns) fails
V8, SpiderMonkey, JavaScriptCore true "a"
formal model (--web-browser, test262 reading) true "a"

Proposed wording

In §B.1.2.2, amend step 1 of CountLeftCapturingParensWithin to allow productions in #sec-patterns or #sec-regular-expressions-patterns.

test262

Every annexB/ RegExp test containing a quantifier exercises CountLeftCapturingParensWithin on an ExtendedAtom or QuantifiableAssertion.
