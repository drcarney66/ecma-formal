The parser's laws

What a model's parser must give, stated in SpecTec with its complete §5.1 / §11.2.2 / §12 derivation closure: a Parse Node related to a derivation of the source text under the grammar (relation Model.NodeRel, laws Model.ParseLaws), a covered node that reparses its cover's matched tokens (laws Model.CoverLaws), and the laws of a Parse Node's children and descendants (laws Model.NodeLaws). All three are proved of the engine (Engine.Claims.parseLaws, Engine.Claims.coverLaws, Engine.Claims.nodeLaws).

Grammar derivations, lexical scanning and ASI (§5.1, §12)

From spec/spectec/prose/04-derives.spectec

Derives is §5.1's derivation relation over GL's notation (Grammar.Core, read through Grammar.Notation), for each grammar and goal symbol, with parameters and guards (§5.1.6), optional symbols (one right-hand side per presence mask, so a node's production is a GL production id), lookahead, [no LineTerminator here], but not and [empty]. Its terminals come from an Alphabet: code points for the lexical, RegExp and numeric string grammars, tokens for the syntactic grammar. The descriptive phrases, <USP> and the [> but only if …] conditions are the alphabet's (b) primitives.

but not and the lookahead ∉ N are negative. Derives is therefore the limit of strata: stratum k + 1 reads the exclusions in stratum k, and a derivation is one at every stratum from some on, which is §5.1's relation for a grammar whose exclusions are stratified, as GL's are.

The rest of §5.1.3, §5.1.5, §11 and §12 is stated over it: scanning source text into input elements (Scans), the tokens and their line terminators, the choice of lexical goal at the parse's position (GoalsAt), automatic semicolon insertion (ASIOk), and chain productions (chainNt).
