Three things to know first

  1. SpecTec and spec.html form a verified bijection. Every algorithm, equation, early-error rule, table and grammar production lives in spec/spectec/ (def @sec-…, grammar, early_error, syntax, field, enum). crates/spectec-html splices spec/spectec/ back into spec.html with zero byte diffs via explicit presentation and patch tables in spec/html/; spectec roundtrip checks SpecTec's own formatting; and scripts/bijection.py prints SpecTec back from downstream Lean byte for byte.
  2. Where the English is prose, SpecTec states syntax, relation and laws — quoting spec.html. What the text assumes (that mathematical values form an ordered field, that Numbers are IEEE 754 binary64) and what it leaves to the host (time, job scheduling, locale, §29 memory) is written in spec/spectec/prose/*.spectec, and every law quotes the governing sentence of spec.html (scripts/law_quotes.py).
  3. The downstream Lean claim is precise and has limits. crates/spectec compiles SpecTec to Lean 4, where the concrete engine and its parser are proved against the specification relying on Lean's {{axiom_count}} standard axioms alone (0 native_decide pins; {{runtime_pins}} shortcut and parser-table checks verified at run time). Every stated SpecTec law is proved; the debugger loop is outside the claim; What is proved lists all of it.
