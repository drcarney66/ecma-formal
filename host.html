The laws of a host

From spec/spectec/prose/26-host-laws.spectec

What the specification requires of the host, stated over a Host and never assumed: a law constrains what one call of the host gives, given the state and the host-call log (State.hostLog) before it. The machine owns the log: a host reads it, and a host step adds only its own entry. Each law is a field of the structure of its topic (Ecma/Host/Laws/), and its docstring says the clause of the specification it reads, quotes the sentence that governs it, and gives its reading (scripts/law_quotes.py checks the quotations against spec.html).

All 72 laws

Each law is a law declaration in the SpecTec laws block of its topic, and Host.WF extends all of them. Two read their sentence more strictly than it is written, and are for the list of proposed normative changes; two more weakly:

  • escapePattern stricter requires \/ and the line terminators' escapes, where the text also allows an unescaped / in a character class.
  • nativeFunctionSource stricter one ASCII form, function name() { [native code] }, where the text allows any NativeFunction.
  • collect weaker approximates liveness by syntactic heap reachability at job boundaries (omitting future-execution observability and cleanup-job scheduling guarantees).
  • agentNextJob weaker there is no law about a timeout.

Which job the host takes next, its preparation and cleanup, the job callbacks, and which agent runs (sec-jobs, sec-agents, sec-forward-progress).
