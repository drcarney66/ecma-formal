How the suite was run

crates/test262-runner runs each test on ecma-run after its harness in both non-strict and strict mode (once for onlyStrict, noStrict, raw, or module), executing the test's own assertions. The 60 s pass runs default proved ecma-run under 60 s of wall clock, a store of 512 MB and 2 GB of memory per test. The 600 s pass reruns the 665 non-passing tests with 600 s of wall clock, a store of 2 GB and 3 GB of memory per test and mark-and-sweep store collection (--gc, outside Engine.implements), adding 129 passes.

Skipped (5,473 tests, tests/test262/suite.conf): runs annexB, built-ins, harness, language, staging (leaving out intl402 and staging/sm), and skips tests gated on features outside the pinned ECMA-262 specification (Temporal, unmerged proposals, ECMA-402, and non-standard caller).
