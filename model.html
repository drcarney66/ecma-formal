The model and its laws

A model (syntax Model record) gives each sort of the specification (mathematical values, Numbers, BigInts, Strings, Symbols, Parse Nodes, Lists, Data Blocks, CharSets, Records, the store) a carrier and its operations, over which specification values (syntax Val) and store cells (syntax Cell) are defined. Every object cites its governing ECMA-262 clause, and the SpecTec laws blocks state what the prose assumes of them: 148 laws, each quoting the sentence it reads.
